Frequently asked

Common questions before applying.

What is the usual maximum tenure for a home loan?

Many lenders offer long tenures, often up to 30 years, subject to age, income, property, and policy conditions.

Can NRIs apply for home loans in India?

Yes, NRIs can apply with eligible documentation, income proof, and lender-specific compliance requirements.

Why does CIBIL score matter?

A strong credit profile can improve approval confidence and may influence the rate or terms offered by a lender.

Is this consultation paid?

No. The consultation offered through this website is presented as a free guidance service.
